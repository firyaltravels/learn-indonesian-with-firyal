A Little About Me

Hi, I'm Firyal.

I'm an Indonesian language tutor and an engineer based in Indonesia. I've had the pleasure of teaching Bahasa Indonesia to international learners from different backgrounds.

My approach combines structured learning with practical communication. With my engineering background, I naturally enjoy breaking down complex concepts into simple, understandable steps.

But language is more than grammar and vocabulary. It's about people, everyday expressions and understanding the culture behind the words.

An engineer by profession, a language tutor by passion.
